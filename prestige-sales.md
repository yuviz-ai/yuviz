# Prestige Real Estate - Sales Guide

## Why Choose Prestige?

Prestige Real Estate represents the pinnacle of luxury living with three exceptional projects:
- **East Wing**: Modern architectural excellence with panoramic city views
- **West Wing**: Serene residential sanctuary with landscape gardens  
- **Penthouses**: Ultra-luxury penthouses with private terraces

Our properties are designed for discerning clients who value quality, location, and investment potential.

## Sales Process

### Step 1: Understand Customer Needs
Ask about:
- Budget range and financial capacity
- Preferred location (East/West/Penthouse)
- Lifestyle preferences (city views, gardens, privacy)
- Timeline for purchase

### Step 2: Present Relevant Project
Based on customer needs, present the most suitable project:
- **East Wing** for clients who want modern city living
- **West Wing** for clients seeking peaceful, garden-oriented spaces
- **Penthouses** for ultra-luxury seekers

### Step 3: Highlight Key Benefits
- Premium location and accessibility
- World-class architecture and design
- Investment value and appreciation potential
- Exclusive community and amenities

### Step 4: Address Concerns
- Payment flexibility and financing options available
- Transparent pricing with no hidden costs
- Possession timeline clearly communicated
- Legal documentation and compliance guaranteed

### Step 5: Move to Next Step
Once interest is confirmed:
- Schedule property visit/virtual tour
- Provide detailed brochure and specifications
- Offer financing consultation
- Arrange meeting with dedicated relationship manager

## Objection Handling

### Price Objection
"The higher price reflects superior location, design, and investment returns. Over 10 years, appreciation alone will justify the premium."

### Timing Objection
"The best time to invest was yesterday. The second best is today. Prices appreciate quarterly, so earlier purchase means better returns."

### Comparison Objection
"Prestige is in a different category. Our appreciation rates, rental yields, and amenities aren't comparable to standard projects."

## Sales Metrics to Track

### Tour Conversion
- Target: 40% of tours should result in booking

### Booking Conversion
- Target: 70% of bookings should result in possession
- Track: Financing status, documentation progress

## Escalation Triggers

Move customer to senior relationship manager if:
- Budget exceeds 10 Cr (complex financing)
- International buyer (legal/documentation complexity)
- Corporate bulk purchase (special pricing needed)
- Custom requirements or modifications requested

## Key Success Factors

1. **Listen First** - Understand customer needs before presenting
2. **Match Project** - Present the property that fits their lifestyle
3. **Build Trust** - Be transparent about pricing, timeline, and risks
4. **Highlight Value** - Focus on benefits, not just features
5. **Facilitate Next Step** - Make scheduling tours/meetings effortless
6. **Follow Up** - Contact prospects within 24 hours of tour/call
7. **Exceed Expectations** - Provide personalized service that creates advocates

Remember: You're not just selling property, you're helping customers achieve their dream lifestyle while making a sound investment.
